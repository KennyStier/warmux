Pick a team. Aim. Boom.

Warmux is a turn-based artillery game starring the mascots of free software. Each turn you get one character, a few seconds and a ridiculous arsenal. Blow holes in the map, knock rivals into the water, and try not to catch yourself in the blast.

Turn-based mayhem

Up to eight teams take turns wrecking fully destructible maps, and each other.

30+ weapons

Bazookas, disco grenades, a stampeding gnu, anvils from the sky and one very smelly polecat.

Online in one click

Public Battle is always open: pick it in the Network Game menu. No port forwarding.

Couch, LAN or phone

Share one screen, find games on your network automatically, or play with touch controls on Android.
